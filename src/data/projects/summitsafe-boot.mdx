## Overview

**SummitSafe** is a wearable sensing system embedded within a winter boot designed to detect early indicators of cold-related injury and intervene before frostbite occurs.

The system integrates:

- In-boot temperature sensing  
- Heart rate monitoring  
- Piezoelectric energy harvesting  
- Haptic feedback via vibration motors  

The goal was to create a low-profile, durable, partially self-sustaining system capable of operating in extreme environments such as mountaineering, skiing, and northern climates.

---

## Prototype

![SummitSafe Prototype](/Portfolio-Website/images/projects/summitsafe/prototype.png)

The prototype integrates all sensing, actuation, and energy-harvesting components directly into the boot lining.

Key integration features:

- LilyPad microcontroller stitched using conductive thread  
- Embedded piezoelectric discs within the sole  
- Temperature sensor positioned inside the boot cavity  
- Dual vibration motors placed for localized circulation stimulation  
- Lithium polymer battery integrated into side lining  

The system maintains flexibility while preserving user comfort and boot integrity.

## System Architecture

![System Architecture](/Portfolio-Website/images/projects/summitsafe/architecture.png)

The wearable system integrates sensing, processing, actuation, and energy harvesting within a compact textile-compatible platform.

### Microcontroller
**Arduino LilyPad**
- Designed for wearable electronics
- Low profile
- Integrated battery interface

### Sensors
- Temperature sensor (ambient in-boot measurement)
- Heart rate sensor (physiological monitoring)

### Actuators
- Dual vibration motors for circulation stimulation

### Energy Subsystem
- Piezoelectric discs (mechanical-to-electrical conversion)
- Diode rectifier for AC-to-DC conversion
- 3.7V 1500mAh LiPo battery

Conductive thread was used to maintain flexibility and waterproof integration.

---

## Energy Harvesting

Piezoelectric discs embedded beneath the foot generate voltage during walking.

Measured output:
- ~170 mV per compression cycle

While insufficient for full system autonomy, the harvested energy provides supplemental charging during prolonged activity.

---

## Detection Logic

The system activates vibration motors when risk conditions are met.

### Temperature Threshold
Motor activates when temperature falls below **22°C**.

### Combined Risk Condition
If:
- Heart rate is detected  
- AND temperature is below threshold  

Then:
- Vibration motors activate to stimulate circulation  

Threshold values are configurable.

## Functional Testing

### Temperature Test
Temperature readings were logged and verified against activation thresholds.

### Heart Rate Test
Sensor successfully detected pulse and triggered motor activation when temperature threshold conditions were met.

### Integrated System Test
- Temperature threshold: 24°C  
- Heart rate threshold: 60 bpm  
- System triggered haptic feedback under defined test conditions

## Engineering Priorities

- Low-profile wearable integration  
- Durability under repeated compression  
- Ease of maintenance  
- Expandability for commercial outdoor applications  

The design avoids bulky heating elements and instead focuses on early detection and circulation stimulation.

---

## Limitations

- Piezoelectric output is supplemental only  
- Ambient temperature measurement rather than tissue temperature  
- Limited field testing in extreme climates  

Future iterations may integrate:
- Improved harvesting efficiency  
- Localized heating elements  
- Wireless telemetry  
- Enhanced physiological modeling
